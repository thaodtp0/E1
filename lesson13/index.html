📌 Grammar 1: like / love / hate / enjoy + V-ing

Sau like, love, hate, enjoy dùng động từ thêm -ing.

I like / love / enjoy + V-ing

I hate + V-ing

(–) I don't like watching horror movies.

(?) Do you enjoy going to the park?

✏️ listen → listening, go → going, get → getting (gấp đôi t)

I love listening to music.
(Tôi rất thích nghe nhạc.)

She hates getting takeout food.
(Cô ấy ghét mua đồ ăn mang đi.)

📌 Grammar 2: Object pronouns

Đại từ tân ngữ đứng sau động từ hoặc giới từ.

I → me   you → you

he → him   she → her   it → it

we → us   they → them

Tom is an actor. I love him.
(Tom là diễn viên. Tôi rất thích anh ấy.)

My friends like soccer. I play with them.
(Bạn tôi thích bóng đá. Tôi chơi cùng họ.)

📌 Grammar 3: so (vì vậy, nên)

reason (lý do), so result (kết quả)

I love music, so I go to a concert every month.

She hates horror movies, so she watches comedies.

(Tôi thích âm nhạc, nên tôi đi xem hòa nhạc mỗi tháng.)

(Cô ấy ghét phim kinh dị, nên cô ấy xem phim hài.)

⚠️ Có dấu phẩy (,) trước so.

🎤 Speaking: Free time activities / Things you like doing

A: What do you like doing in your free time?

B: I love listening to music and I enjoy watching movies.

A: Do you like sports?

B: Yes, I like soccer. I love watching it on TV. I hate playing tennis, so I don't play it.

Ask and answer with a partner.

📝 Writing: Favorite sports, music and movies (using so)

My name is Nam. In my free time, I love listening to pop music, so I go to a concert every month. My favorite sport is soccer. I enjoy watching the players on TV. I like action movies, but I hate horror movies, so I never watch them.

Use like / love / hate / enjoy + V-ing, object pronouns and so.
Dùng like / love / hate / enjoy + V-ing, đại từ tân ngữ và so.

💡 Tóm tắt nội dung bài học / Lesson summary:

  • 1. Recreational activities (Hoạt động giải trí): get takeout food, go to a concert, go to a soccer game, go to a restaurant, listen to music, watch TV, spend time with family / friends, go to the park / a museum...
  • 2. Entertainment (Giải trí): music (classical, jazz, pop, rock, band...), sports (tennis, soccer, baseball, basketball, stadium...), movies (horror, action, sci-fi, comedy, actor...).
  • 3. like / love / hate / enjoy + V-ing: Ví dụ: I love listening to music. She enjoys going to the park.
  • 4. Object pronouns (Đại từ tân ngữ): me, you, him, her, it, us, them. Ví dụ: I love him. Play with us.
  • 5. so (nên, vì vậy): nối kết quả. Ví dụ: I love music, so I go to a concert every month.
