📌 Grammar 1: Simple present

I / you / we / they + cook, play, go
he / she + cooks, plays, goes
No / không: I don't play chess. She doesn't play chess.
Question: Do you ...? / Does he ...?

He goes to the gym on Monday.
(Anh ấy đi tập gym vào thứ Hai.)

Do you cook a meal at home?
(Bạn có nấu ăn ở nhà không?)

📌 Grammar 2: Adverbs of frequency

never (0%) → seldom → often → frequently → always (100%)
không bao giờ → hiếm khi → thường → thường xuyên → luôn luôn

Before a main verb: I often go shopping.
After be: She is always busy.

I always have breakfast at home.
(Tôi luôn ăn sáng ở nhà.)

He never eats broccoli.
(Anh ấy không bao giờ ăn bông cải xanh.)

🎤 Speaking: Free-time activities

A: What do you do in your free time?
B: I often go for a walk. I seldom play chess.
A: How often do you go shopping?
B: I go shopping twice a month.

Ask and answer with a partner.
