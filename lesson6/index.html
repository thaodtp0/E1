📌 Grammar 1: There is / There are

There is + a / an + singular noun (1 cái)

There are + plural noun (nhiều cái)

No / không: There isn't a... / There aren't any...

There is a park in my town.
(Có một công viên trong thị trấn của tôi.)

There are two hotels near the station.
(Có hai khách sạn gần nhà ga.)

📌 Grammar 2: Some / Any

some → câu khẳng định (+)

any → câu phủ định (–) và câu hỏi (?)

There are some chairs in the kitchen.
(Có vài cái ghế trong bếp.)

There aren't any mirrors in the bathroom.
(Không có cái gương nào trong phòng tắm.)

Are there any museums in your city?
(Thành phố bạn có bảo tàng nào không?)

📌 Grammar 3: Is there...? / Are there...?

Is there + a / an + singular noun?

Are there + any + plural noun?

Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.

Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

Is there a fridge in the kitchen? – Yes, there is.
(Có tủ lạnh trong bếp không? – Có.)

Are there any hotels near the park? – No, there aren't.
(Có khách sạn nào gần công viên không? – Không.)

📌 Grammar 4: in / on / under & because

in = trong   on = trên   under = dưới

because + reason (bởi vì + lý do)

The book is on the desk. The shoes are under the bed.
(Quyển sách ở trên bàn. Đôi giày ở dưới giường.)

I like this cafe because it is cheap.
(Tôi thích quán cà phê này vì nó rẻ.)

🎤 Speaking 1: Places for everyday life, weekend fun, food and drinks

A: What places are there in your town?

B: There is a big park and there are two cafes.

A: Is there a museum?

B: Yes, there is. I go there at the weekend.

📝 Writing | Review: A review of a cafe

Sunny Cafe is my favorite cafe. I like it because it is cheap. The coffee is good because it is fresh. There are some tables near the window. I go there at the weekend.

Use “because” in your sentences.
Dùng “because” trong câu của bạn.
