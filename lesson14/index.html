📌 Grammar 1: Countable / uncountable nouns

Countable / đếm được: a ticket, two tickets, a tent, many bags
→ use a / an, numbers, many
Uncountable / không đếm được: money, luggage, traffic
→ use some, much (no -s, no a / an)
a lot of + both types

I have a passport and some money.
(Tôi có một hộ chiếu và một ít tiền.)

We don't have much luggage.
(Chúng tôi không có nhiều hành lý.)

📌 Grammar 2: Present progressive for future plans

I am + V-ing · he / she is + V-ing · we / you / they are + V-ing
+ future time: tomorrow, next week, next month, on Saturday
Question: Are you going to Da Nang next week?

We are flying to Da Nang next week.
(Tuần sau chúng tôi sẽ bay đến Đà Nẵng.)

She is taking a camera.
(Cô ấy sẽ mang theo một chiếc máy ảnh.)

🚌 Travel + by / Đi bằng phương tiện gì

by + vehicle: by bike, by bus, by car, by plane, by taxi, by train, by trolley
on foot = walking (không dùng "by foot")

I go to the airport by taxi. I go to school on foot.
(Tôi đến sân bay bằng taxi. Tôi đi bộ đến trường.)

📝 Writing: About your next vacation

I'm going on a beach vacation next month. I'm traveling by plane. I'm taking a camera, sunglasses and some money. I'm not taking much luggage.

Use the present progressive for your plans.
