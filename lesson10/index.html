📌 Dates and years / Ngày tháng và năm

Cardinal numbers (số đếm): 1 one, 2 two, 3 three ... 12 twelve, 20 twenty, 21 twenty-one, 30 thirty, 31 thirty-one

Ordinal numbers (số thứ tự): 1st first, 2nd second, 3rd third, 4th fourth, 5th fifth, 8th eighth, 9th ninth, 12th twelfth, 20th twentieth, 21st twenty-first, 30th thirtieth, 31st thirty-first

✏️ Số thứ tự thường = số đếm + th (four → fourth). Chú ý: first, second, third, eighth, ninth, twelfth.

Dates (ngày): on + the + ordinal + of + month

My birthday is on the second of June. (June 2nd)

Years (năm): in + year. Đọc 2 số một:

1990 → nineteen ninety

1905 → nineteen oh five

2000 → two thousand

2015 → twenty fifteen

📌 Grammar 1: Simple past of be (was / were)

I / he / she / it + was

you / we / they + were

(–) wasn't / weren't

(?) Was he ...? / Were they ...?

Yes, he was. / No, they weren't.

She was a famous scientist.
(Bà ấy là một nhà khoa học nổi tiếng.)

We weren't at home yesterday.
(Hôm qua chúng tôi không ở nhà.)

Was he a singer? – Yes, he was.
(Anh ấy là ca sĩ phải không? – Đúng vậy.)

📌 Grammar 2: Simple past of regular verbs

(+) V + -ed: help → helped, paint → painted

Verb tận cùng -e: + d: design → designed, receive → received

Verb tận cùng phụ âm + y: study → studied

(–) didn't + V: She didn't paint.

(?) Did you + V ...? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

He collected stamps in 2015.
(Anh ấy đã sưu tầm tem vào năm 2015.)

Did she study in college? – Yes, she did.
(Cô ấy đã học đại học phải không? – Đúng vậy.)

🎤 Speaking: A famous person

A: Who was Marie Curie?

B: She was a famous scientist.

A: When was she born?

B: She was born in eighteen sixty-seven.

A: What did she do?

B: She studied in Paris and received a prize in 1903.

Ask and answer about a famous person you like.
Hỏi và trả lời về một người nổi tiếng mà bạn thích.

📝 Writing: A famous person

Marie Curie was a famous scientist. She was born in 1867. She studied in Paris and helped many people. She received a prize in 1903. She was an amazing woman.

Use was / were, regular past verbs, and dates or years.
Dùng was / were, động từ có quy tắc ở quá khứ, và ngày hoặc năm.

💡 Tóm tắt nội dung bài học / Lesson summary:

  • 1. Dates and years (Ngày tháng và năm): số đếm 1–31 (one, two, three...), số thứ tự (first, second, third, fourth, fifth, twelfth, twentieth, thirty-first...). Năm: 1990 = nineteen ninety, 2000 = two thousand, 2015 = twenty fifteen.
  • 2. Everyday verbs (Động từ thông dụng): collect stamps, design a building, help a friend, paint a picture, receive a present, start to run, study in college, travel to the city.
  • 3. Simple past: was / were: I / he / she / it was; you / we / they were. Ví dụ: She was a scientist. Were they happy?
  • 4. Simple past: regular verbs (Động từ có quy tắc): V + -ed (helped, painted, studied). Phủ định / nghi vấn dùng didn't / Did + V.
