📋 Lesson Summary / Tóm tắt kiến thức

  • 🏃 Daily activities / Hoạt động hằng ngày: have breakfast, go home, go to bed, watch TV / a movie, talk to my friends / family, read a book.

  • 📅 Months and seasons (NAmE) / Tháng và mùa: January ... December; spring, summer, fall, winter.
  • ⏰ Time expressions / Cụm chỉ thời gian: in the morning / evening, each week / month, at + hour.
  • ❓ Wh-questions / Câu hỏi Wh: what, where, when, how, what time, how far.
  • ✍️ Grammar / Ngữ pháp: the simple present for routines.
  • 🎯 Goal / Mục tiêu: talk and write about a day (your daily routine).
    Nói và viết về một ngày của bạn (thói quen hằng ngày).

📌 Grammar 1: Simple present for routines

I / you / we / they + have, go, watch
he / she + has, goes, watches
Question: Do you ...? / Does she ...?

I have breakfast at 7:00 in the morning.
(Tôi ăn sáng lúc 7 giờ vào buổi sáng.)

She goes to bed at 10:30 p.m.
(Cô ấy đi ngủ lúc 10 giờ 30 tối.)

📌 Grammar 2: Time expressions

in the morning / the evening
at + hour: at seven o'clock, at 7:15
each week / each month

I read a book in the evening.
(Tôi đọc sách vào buổi tối.)

We talk to our family each week.
(Chúng tôi nói chuyện với gia đình mỗi tuần.)

🕖 Tell the time (NAmE) / Nói giờ

7:00 → seven o'clock
7:15 → seven fifteen
7:30 → seven thirty
7:45 → seven forty-five
a.m. = morning (sáng) · p.m. = afternoon / night (chiều, tối)

In North American English, say the hour first, then the minutes.
Trong tiếng Anh Mỹ, đọc giờ trước rồi đến phút.

❓ Wh-questions / Câu hỏi Wh

What time do you have breakfast?
Where do you have lunch?
When do you go to bed?
How do you go home?
How far is your school?

Wh-word + do / does + subject + verb.
Từ để hỏi Wh + do / does + chủ ngữ + động từ.

🎤 Speaking: A day

I get up at 6:30 a.m. I have breakfast at seven o'clock in the morning. I go home at 5:00 p.m. In the evening, I watch TV and read a book. I go to bed at 10:30 p.m.

Write short sentences with times and time expressions.
