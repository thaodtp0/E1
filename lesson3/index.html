📌 Grammar 1: this / that / these / those

Near / gần: this (1) · these (2+)
Far / xa: that (1) · those (2+)

This is my mother.
(Đây là mẹ tôi.)

Those are my parents.
(Kia là bố mẹ tôi.)

📌 Grammar 2: Singular / plural nouns

1 bag → 2 bags · 1 hat → 2 hats
1 sister → 2 sisters
Always plural / luôn số nhiều: glasses, headphones, parents, grandparents

I have two brothers.
(Tôi có hai anh trai.)

These are my glasses.
(Đây là kính của tôi.)

📌 Grammar 3: have / has

I / you / we / they + have
he / she / it + has
my sister / my father + has

I have a red hat.
(Tôi có một cái mũ đỏ.)

My sister has a black bag.
(Chị tôi có một cái túi đen.)

🎤 Speaking: Objects and family

A: What's this? (Đây là gì?)
B: It's a blue hat.
A: Who's that? (Kia là ai?)
B: That's my father. He has a gray coat.

Practice with a partner. Point to objects and people in your class.
Luyện tập với bạn bên cạnh. Chỉ vào đồ vật và người trong lớp.

📝 Writing: Family members

I have a big family. This is my father. He has a black phone. This is my mother. She has a white bag. I have two brothers and a sister. Those are my grandparents.

Write short sentences about each family member and their objects.
