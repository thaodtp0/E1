📋 Lesson Summary / Tóm tắt kiến thức

  • ⏳ Time phrases / Cụm chỉ thời gian: in + year, last week / year, ... ago, in the past, a long time ago.
  • 🎓 Life events / Sự kiện cuộc sống: pass a test, meet a person, finish college, write a story, belong to sb, get married, win a prize, drive a car.
  • 🔄 Sequencing words / Từ chỉ trình tự: then, after, later, next.
  • ✍️ Grammar / Ngữ pháp: the simple past (irregular verbs) and simple past questions.
  • 🎯 Goal / Mục tiêu: talk about an important object and past experiences; write about a happy memory.
    Nói về một đồ vật quan trọng và những trải nghiệm trong quá khứ; viết về một kỷ niệm vui.

📌 Grammar 1: Simple past (irregular verbs)

go → went · have → had · see → saw
take → took · make → made · buy → bought
eat → ate · come → came · give → gave
meet → met · write → wrote · get → got
win → won · drive → drove · say → said

I won a prize in 2020.
(Tôi đã giành giải thưởng vào năm 2020.)

Irregular verbs do not end in -ed. Learn them by heart.
Động từ bất quy tắc không thêm -ed. Hãy học thuộc.

📌 Grammar 2: Simple past questions

Did + subject + base verb?
Did you win a prize? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Wh-word + did + subject + base verb?
Where did he go last week?

Did she finish college in 2015?
(Cô ấy tốt nghiệp đại học năm 2015 phải không?)

After did, use the base verb (go, not went).
Sau did, dùng động từ nguyên mẫu (go, không dùng went).

⏳ Time phrases / Cụm chỉ thời gian

in + year: in 2020
last + time: last week, last year
time + ago: two days ago
in the past · a long time ago

We met two days ago.
(Chúng tôi đã gặp nhau hai ngày trước.)

🔄 Sequencing words / Từ chỉ trình tự

I wrote a story. Then, I gave it to my teacher.
After that, I called my mother.
Later, we had dinner.
Next, we watched a movie.

Put a comma after then, later and next at the start of a sentence.
Đặt dấu phẩy sau then, later, next ở đầu câu.

🎤 Speaking: An important object and past experiences

A: What is an important object to you?
B: My watch. My father gave it to me in 2020.
A: Did you win a prize last year?
B: Yes, I did. I won a prize at school.

Ask and answer with a partner.
Hỏi và trả lời với bạn bên cạnh.

📝 Writing: A happy memory

Last year, I wrote a story for my English class. Then, I gave it to my teacher. Later, I won a prize. After that, I called my mother. Next, we had dinner together. I was very happy.

Use sequencing words in your story.
Dùng từ chỉ trình tự trong câu chuyện của bạn.
